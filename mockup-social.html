Why I'm Bullish on ASML Going Into Q3

CS

Carlos Suárez

@carlos-suarez · April 3, 2026 · 5 min read

ASML continues to dominate EUV lithography with no credible competitor on the horizon. Their backlog now stretches to 2028, and the recent earnings beat suggests pricing power remains intact.

📊 Embedded chart: ASML Revenue & Backlog 2020-2028E

Key Thesis Points:

1. Monopoly position in EUV — ASML is the sole supplier of extreme ultraviolet lithography machines. TSMC, Samsung, and Intel all depend on them for advanced node production.

2. Pricing power — The latest High-NA EUV systems cost over €350M each, and customers are lining up. This isn't a commodity business.

3. Secular tailwinds — AI chip demand, automotive semiconductors, and the ongoing shift to advanced nodes all drive ASML's addressable market higher.

At current valuations (~32x forward earnings), ASML isn't cheap, but it rarely is. Quality comes at a premium, and this is one of the highest-quality businesses in the world.

⚠ Disclaimer: This is not financial advice. I hold a position in ASML. Always do your own research before making investment decisions.
