Portfolio Overview

View State

Trigger Logic

  • Show after 8 meaningful interactions (holding add, import, AI review, export, alert, etc.)
  • Minimum 3 days since account creation
  • If dismissed → reset counter, re-show after 15 more interactions
  • If submitted → never show again (DB flag)
  • Max 3 lifetime dismissals → stop asking forever

Non-Invasive Design

  • Bottom-right slide-up widget, not a blocking modal
  • No backdrop — dashboard stays fully usable
  • Close button always visible, Escape key works
  • Never shown during active workflows (import, checkout, onboarding)
  • Small footprint: 360px wide, compact height

Trustpilot Evaluation

★ Recommended
  • Pros: Strong in EU market (your users), social proof for landing page, SEO boost, trusted by investors
  • Cons: €200-900/mo for business tools; free tier shows reviews only
  • Strategy: Redirect only 4-5★ users to Trustpilot (deflection pattern). Keep low ratings internal.
  • Start free: Claim your Trustpilot profile + add TrustBox widget to landing page. Upgrade later if needed.
