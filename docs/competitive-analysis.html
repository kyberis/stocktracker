Competitor UI/UX Deep Dives

Simply Wall St — The Visual Pioneer
Snowflake Infographic Model

Simply Wall St's defining innovation is the “Snowflake” — a radial chart scoring companies on 5 axes (valuation, growth, past performance, health, dividends) with 6 checks each. Color shifts from red to green based on score.

Strengths: Transforms intimidating financial data into instant visual comprehension. One-page company reports enable sub-60-second stock evaluation. Pre-set screener categories ("trending", "undervalued") lower the discovery barrier.

Weaknesses: Desktop-heavy design feels dated on mobile. Information density can overwhelm beginners despite the visual approach. Freemium gates are aggressive (5 reports/month).

Lesson for trefolio: Visual summarization of complex data is powerful. Consider a visual health score for holdings.

Stock Events — The Mobile Benchmark
Calendar-Centric, Beautiful Mobile UX

4.8 stars on both stores. The UI is calendar-first — dividends, earnings, IPOs, and economic events are the primary navigation paradigm. Every screen feels purpose-built for mobile.

Strengths: Best-in-class mobile design. Home screen widgets that actually work well. Clean typography, generous whitespace, no visual clutter. The calendar model creates daily engagement hooks.

Weaknesses: Lighter on analytics depth. No AI features. Limited broker auto-sync. Web version is secondary.

Lesson for trefolio: Event calendars drive daily opens. Mobile widget polish is worth significant investment. Simplicity wins ratings.

Delta (eToro) — The Cross-Platform Standard
Sleek, Consistent Multi-Device Experience

Described as “the most aesthetically pleasing” tracker app. Charts below price displays add visual character. Seamless sync across iOS, Android, Windows, Mac, and web via QR code.

Strengths: Professional, modern UI. Deep customization (sorting, display, multi-portfolio). Recent AI additions ("What's going on?", "Delta Direct") show smart integration of contextual AI.

Weaknesses: Users report functional bugs — fee miscalculations, duplicate transactions, notification failures. Beautiful design undermined by data accuracy issues. Crypto-first heritage can feel off for stock-only investors.

Lesson for trefolio: Design consistency across platforms matters. Data accuracy > visual polish. Delta's contextual AI ("explain my portfolio movement") is a pattern worth adopting.

Getquin — The Social Incumbent
Community-Driven Portfolio Sharing

4.7 stars. UI is clean and information-dense without feeling cluttered. The social layer (forums, anonymous portfolio sharing, community benchmarks) is the primary differentiator and engagement driver.

Strengths: 30K+ bank connections make onboarding nearly frictionless. DeepDive AI analysis. Retirement planning module justifies the premium Wealth tier (€150/yr). Net worth tracking goes beyond investments.

Weaknesses: Users frustrated by feature lock-ins across too many tiers. Credential storage raises privacy concerns. Social features can feel noisy for users who just want tracking. Not available in as many languages.

Lesson for trefolio: Social proof and community are powerful retention tools. But social should be opt-in, not forced. Bank connection breadth is the #1 onboarding advantage.

Snowball Analytics — The Power User’s Choice
Feature-Rich Analytics Platform

4.8–4.9 stars. Described as “beautiful and powerful”. Home page dashboard shows IRR, passive income, total profit, and portfolio value at a glance. Supports widgets.

Strengths: 30-year backtesting is unique. Broad asset coverage (real estate, bonds, custom). Dividend analytics with visual graphs and calendar. Goal tracking with progress charts.

Weaknesses: Steep learning curve for new users. Real-time pricing can lag. Expensive ($80–$250/yr). Feature density means the UI can feel overwhelming.

Lesson for trefolio: Power features must be progressively disclosed, not dumped on the user. Backtesting is a high-value feature that few competitors offer.

Sharesight — The Tax Specialist
Tax Reporting as the Primary Value Prop

Functional rather than flashy. The UI is report-driven — tables, PDFs, and export-ready tax documents. Designed for the investor who needs numbers to be correct above all else.

Strengths: Country-specific tax reports (AU, NZ, US, UK, CA). 20-year automatic history. SOC 2 compliant. Advisor dashboard for professionals. 200+ broker connections. Most trusted for accuracy.

Weaknesses: Dated visual design. No mobile-first experience. No AI. Premium pricing ($84–$372/yr). Not available in multiple languages.

Lesson for trefolio: Tax reporting is the #1 upgrade driver for serious investors. Country-specific EU tax reports are an unoccupied niche that could be trefolio's Sharesight-equivalent moat in Europe.
