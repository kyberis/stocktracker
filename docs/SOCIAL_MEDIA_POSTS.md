## Build-in-Public Thread (Post Day 2-3)

### Thread: How I Built trefolio

🧵 How I built a portfolio tracker for European investors — from scratch to v0.50

**1/** I started building trefolio because I was tracking my stocks in Google Sheets. It worked until I had 20+ positions across DEGIRO and IBKR, in EUR and USD. The spreadsheet became unmaintainable.

**2/** I looked at existing trackers: Simply Wall St ($10/mo, no DEGIRO import), Seeking Alpha ($20/mo, US-focused), Portfolio Performance (free but desktop-only with a steep learning curve). None fit.

**3/** So I built my own. Tech stack: Next.js 14, Turso (libSQL), Stripe, Vercel. Market data from Yahoo Finance (free tier) and Alpha Vantage (Pro). AI analysis via OpenAI.

**4/** The hardest part was broker imports. Every broker has a different CSV format, different column names, different date formats. DEGIRO's CSV has 20+ transaction types. It took weeks to get parsing right.

**5/** Multi-currency was the second hardest. When you buy a US stock from a EUR account on DEGIRO, the CSV has multiple lines: the trade, the FX conversion, and the fee. All need to be linked.

**6/** AI analysis surprised me — it's the feature users like most. You ask "analyze ASML" and get a comprehensive breakdown of the business, financials, valuation, and risks. In plain language, in your language.

**7/** I added 35 languages because Europe deserves tools in local languages. The AI responds in the user's language too — portfolio analysis in Finnish, Portuguese, or Bulgarian.

**8/** Current state: v0.50, free tier (15 holdings), Bifolio at €2.99/month, Trefolio at €7.99/month. Still a solo project, still learning every day.

Try it: https://trefolio.com
