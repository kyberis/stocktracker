Tier Recommendation

Net worth tracking is a high-perceived-value, low-development-cost feature. Placing it at Starter (Bifolio) tier maximizes conversion from Free users at the impulse-buy price point (€2.99/mo), while keeping Pro for power features. Cash entries remain free for all tiers.

Rationale

  • Why Starter, not Pro? — The feature is manual data entry with no external API cost. Gating it at Pro (€7.99/mo) would be a high barrier for a feature that is fundamentally simple. Starter at €2.99/mo is below the impulse-buy threshold and directly competes with Getquin Free → Premium upsell.
  • Why not Free? — Cash entries (the current model) stay free. But full net worth tracking with categorised asset types, a dedicated overview, and multi-currency support is a material upgrade. Keeping it paid protects the value perception and gives Free users a clear reason to convert.
  • Limit strategy — Starter gets up to 10 manual assets (enough for most users: 1-2 properties, 2-3 savings, 1-2 pensions). Pro gets unlimited, which matters for HNW users with many properties/accounts.
  • Future upsell — Pro-only features like valuation history charts (track your home value over time) and net worth growth charts create a natural upgrade path from Starter to Pro.
  • Competitor benchmarks — Getquin charges €150/yr for Wealth tier (net worth + retirement). Kubera starts at $249/yr. Even our Pro at €95.88/yr significantly undercuts both. Starter at €35.88/yr is a steal.
